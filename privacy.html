Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

GulfRozgar is a free job-listing website. We keep data collection to the absolute minimum.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: if you sign in, we receive your name and email address from Google (via Firebase Authentication) so you can post reviews. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Reviews: reviews you write (name, star rating, text) are stored in our database and shown publicly on the site.
  • Analytics (optional): we may use Google Analytics to understand anonymous, aggregated usage (pages visited, device type). No advertising profiles are built.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • We never ask for money, bank details, or passport copies. Anyone asking for these in our name is a scammer — report them.

Your control

Want your review removed? Message us on WhatsApp and we will delete it. You can also sign out at any time using the button in the header.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reach us via the WhatsApp button on the Contact section of our homepage.
